Figure 3: AMECF Heterogeneous Context Attention Layer Computation
================================================================================
Description:
Computation graph of the AMECF architecture:
1. Type-specific linear projection heads W_t projecting input vectors into shared hidden space R^32.
2. Heterogeneous Graph Transformer (HGT) multi-head attention computing relational query, key, value, and message tensors across heterogeneous neighborhoods.
3. Target account node representation aggregation followed by 2-layer MLP classifier head with Dropout (p=0.3).
